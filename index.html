About me

Hi, I'm Marie from Moijs.art!

My name is Marie. I am an artist, web designer and game developer, currently studying Multimedia Design. I am naturally curious and always eager to explore new ideas, develop new skills, and take on creative challenges.

My skill set is broad, ranging from illustration and storytelling to web and multimedia design. While I primarily work with digital media, I also enjoy traditional tools and techniques.
